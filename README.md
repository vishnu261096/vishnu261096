# STICK CARNAGE 🩸 (18+ — EXTREME VIOLENCE)

A brutal stickman fighting game with full blood & gore — built in pure HTML5 canvas + JavaScript, zero dependencies.

**▶ Play it: open `index.html` in any browser. That's it.**

> **TESTING BUILD: all 50 levels are unlocked in Level Select.**

## Features

- **40 main levels + 10 bonus blood-frenzy arenas** (50 total) across 10 themed zones:
  Training Grounds → Neon City → Crimson Forest → Frozen Wastes → Scorched Desert →
  Toxic Sewers → Shadow Temple → Sky Fortress → Hell Gates → The Void
- **Every level ends with a named boss** — 50 unique bosses with 8 attack patterns and
  the multi-pattern final god **DEATH INCARNATE** on level 50. Bosses **enrage** at half HP.
- **A new power after every level — 50 powers total**, including 7 evolving special attacks:
  Shockwave → Fireball → Lightning Storm → Blade Tornado → Meteor Call → Black Hole → **APOCALYPSE**
- **16-weapon arsenal**, unlocked as you climb the levels:
  Fists → Knife → Spiked Bat → Machete → Sword → War Axe → Spear → Pistol → Uzi →
  Shotgun → Katana → Grenades → Flamethrower → Laser Rifle → Chainsaw.
  Every level also sharpens your whole arsenal (+4% weapon damage per level past its unlock).
- **MAXIMUM GORE**: blade kills **decapitate** — the head rolls away while the headless body
  stands spraying an arterial fountain, then crumples. Gun **headshots pop heads**. Bodies get
  **sliced in half**. Grenades and chainsaws turn waves into blood mist. Bloodstains soak the
  floor permanently. Screen shake, hit-stop and **slow-motion kill cam** on big kills.
- **Enemies feel fear** — witnesses of brutal kills scream ("AAAH!", "RUN!!") and flee in panic.
- **Kung-fu combat**: proper guard stance, snapping straight punches, high roundhouse kicks,
  **flying jump kick (1.8x damage)**, and **double-tap combos** — tap punch or kick twice fast
  for a double strike with bonus damage.
- Kill-streak rewards: TRIPLE KILL → BLOODBATH → MASSACRE → GENOCIDE, with cinematic slow-mo.
- 8 enemy types, combos, crits, lifesteal, second wind, double/triple jump, dash i-frames,
  synthesized sound effects (no audio files), progress auto-saved in your browser.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| A / D or ← / → | Move |
| W / ↑ / Space | Jump (double & triple jump unlockable) |
| J or Z | Attack with weapon (hold for auto weapons) — **double-tap = double strike** |
| K or X | Kung-fu kick — **double-tap = double kick**, **in mid-air = flying kick (1.8x)** |
| Q / E | Switch weapon (or number keys 1–9, 0) |
| L or C | Special attack (uses energy) |
| Shift | Dash (unlockable, brief invincibility) |
| P / Esc | Pause |
| M | Mute |

## Tips

- Blades decapitate; guns headshot if the bullet hits head height; explosives paint the walls.
- Jump-kicking into a crowd does 1.8x damage and sends bodies flying.
- Energy builds over time and from kills. Specials nuke whole waves.
- Bosses telegraph attacks by flashing — dash through them with i-frames.
- Gold levels on the select screen are bonus blood arenas (+50% damage, free specials).
