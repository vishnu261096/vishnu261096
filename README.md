# STICK CARNAGE 🩸

A brutal stickman fighting game with full blood & gore — built in pure HTML5 canvas + JavaScript, zero dependencies.

**▶ Play it: open `index.html` in any browser. That's it.**

## Features

- **40 main levels + 10 bonus blood-frenzy arenas** (50 total) across 10 themed zones:
  Training Grounds → Neon City → Crimson Forest → Frozen Wastes → Scorched Desert →
  Toxic Sewers → Shadow Temple → Sky Fortress → Hell Gates → The Void
- **Every level ends with a named boss** — 50 unique bosses with 8 attack patterns
  (chargers, slammers, gunners, summoners, teleporters, spinners, laser bosses, and the
  multi-pattern final god **DEATH INCARNATE** on level 50). Bosses **enrage** at half HP.
- **A new power after every level — 50 powers total**, including 7 evolving special attacks:
  Shockwave → Fireball → Lightning Storm → Blade Tornado → Meteor Call → Black Hole → **APOCALYPSE**
- **Maximum gore**: directional blood spray, permanent floor bloodstains, full dismemberment
  (heads & limbs fly off and bounce), screen shake, hit-stop, kill text
- **Bonus arenas**: +50% damage, free specials, enemy swarms
- 8 enemy types: grunts, runners, brutes, ninjas, gunners, suicide bombers, mages, shield bearers
- Combo system, crits, lifesteal, second wind, double/triple jump, dash with i-frames
- Synthesized sound effects (no audio files), progress auto-saved in your browser

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| A / D or ← / → | Move |
| W / ↑ / Space | Jump (double & triple jump unlockable) |
| J or Z | Punch |
| K or X | Kick (heavy) |
| L or C | Special attack (uses energy) |
| Shift | Dash (unlockable, brief invincibility) |
| P / Esc | Pause |
| M | Mute |

## Tips

- Kicks hit harder and knock enemies back; punches are faster — mix them for combos.
- Energy builds over time and from kills. Specials nuke whole waves.
- Bosses telegraph attacks by flashing — dash through them with i-frames.
- Gold levels on the select screen are bonus blood arenas. Level 50 is the end of everything.
